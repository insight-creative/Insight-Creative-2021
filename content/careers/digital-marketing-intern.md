## Digital Marketing Intern

Insight Creative, Inc. is seeking a dynamic and motivated Digital Marketing Intern to join our innovative marketing team. This paid internship offers a fantastic opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of digital marketing. As a Digital Marketing Intern, you will support the development and execution of online campaigns, assist with content creation, and analyze digital performance metrics. This role is perfect for someone passionate about digital trends, eager to learn, and ready to contribute to impactful marketing strategies that drive brand growth and engagement.

## Responsibilities

* Assist in creating engaging content for social media platforms, blogs, email campaigns, and website updates using content management systems like WordPress.

* Support SEO efforts by conducting keyword research, optimizing website content with SEO tools, and implementing on-page SEO best practices.

* Help manage social media marketing campaigns across platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter to increase brand visibility and audience engagement.

* Contribute to performance marketing initiatives by setting up and monitoring Google Ads (formerly Google AdWords), Facebook Advertising, and SEM campaigns.

* Analyze website traffic and campaign performance using tools like Google Analytics and provide actionable insights for optimization.

* Collaborate with the team on conversion optimization strategies to improve landing pages and user experience.

* Assist in email marketing campaigns by creating compelling copy, segmenting audiences, and tracking engagement metrics.

* Support graphic design tasks.

* Participate in brainstorming sessions for new marketing ideas and stay updated on the latest digital marketing trends.

## Experience

* Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration or a related field.

* Familiarity with SEO tools, Google Analytics, Google Ads, Facebook Advertising, and marketing automation platforms is preferred.

* Basic understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL or Content Management Systems such as WordPress is advantageous.

* Experience with graphic design software like Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Creative Suite is a plus.

* Strong written communication skills with an eye for detail in copywriting and content creation.

* Demonstrated interest in digital marketing channels including social media management, SEM, B2B marketing, and e-commerce strategies.

* Ability to analyze data effectively to inform decision-making and optimize campaign performance. This paid internship provides valuable real-world experience while working alongside passionate professionals dedicated to innovation and growth. If you're eager to learn new skills in a vibrant environment that values creativity and results-driven thinking, we want to hear from you!
