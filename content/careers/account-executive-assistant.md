## Marketing & Account Executive Assistant

Grow your career at Insight Creative, one of Northeastern Wisconsin’s leading advertising agencies. With 35+ years of experience, we help clients across a variety of industries grow their businesses.

As a Marketing & Account Executive Assistant, you’ll work with a talented, down-to-earth team to support client accounts, coordinate projects from concept to completion, and ensure exceptional client service.

## Qualifications

A degree in marketing, business, or a related field and at least one year of experience are preferred. Advertising agency experience is a plus.
  
* Strong written, verbal, proofreading, and grammar skills.

* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage multiple projects and deadlines.

* Excellent interpersonal skills and a collaborative, team-oriented approach.

* Self-motivated, quick to learn, and able to work independently.

* Strong decision-making skills and a positive, can-do attitude.

* Proficient in Microsoft Office and general computer applications.

* Familiarity with marketing concepts and terminology.

## Key Responsibilities

### Client Service & Communication

* Support Account Executives with daily client needs, communications, research, and follow-up.

* Attend client briefings, document meetings, and distribute change orders.

* Monitor project progress and keep clients and team members informed.

* Assist with proposals, presentations, pitches, and new business development.

### Project & Workflow Management

* Open new jobs, initiate estimates, organize digital files, and coordinate production.

* Track project timelines, budgets, creative approvals, and deadlines.

* Coordinate with creative, traffic, media, and production teams to keep projects moving.

* Proofread copy and artwork before production and coordinate client approvals.

### Media & Reporting

* Coordinate delivery of broadcast, digital, and outdoor advertising materials.

* Monitor media deadlines and reporting schedules.

* Review digital reports and media invoices for accuracy before distribution or payment.

### Billing & Administrative Support

* Assist with client invoicing, billing updates, invoice tracking, and job scheduling.

* Prepare client communications, marketing plans, media plans, public relations plans, agendas, and other account materials.

* Provide backup support for Account Executives and assist social media and media teams as needed.
